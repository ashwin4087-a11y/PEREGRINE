## 1. Slide 6 — Correction: Removal of Fabricated Planner Multiplier

### The Defect in Previous Deck
Previous Slide 6 displayed misleading **"89"** and **"56"** "All + plan" bars. 
Code tracing in `src/model/graph.ts` revealed these were generated by an arbitrary formula:
$$\text{planResilience} = \min(100, \text{resilienceScore} \times 1.03)$$
This multiplier artificially inflated planner scores, decoupled them from actual intervention selections, and fabricated unrealistic outcomes.

### Corrected Values (From Pure Model Simulation)

#### S30 Alert Window (30 Minutes, Severe Cyclone Scenario)
- **Cell Only (Severed Fibre)**: **0.00%**
- **S30 Baseline (Gateway Operational, Mesh Only)**: **54.0%** (53.95% exact; 10,736.6 / 19,900 population-weighted reach)
  - *Mathematical Derivation & Audit*: The naive model assumed 1 hop for all non-gateway mesh nodes, giving $10,797.6 / 19,900 = \mathbf{54.26\%}$. The authoritative model calculates Euclidean link distances and BFS multi-hop propagation: Marina (0 hops, 0.3 min), Kovalam/Pazhaya/Karai (1 hop, 0.8 min), Uppalam (2 hops, 1.3 min), and Sagar (3 hops, 1.8 min), giving $10,736.6 / 19,900 = \mathbf{53.95\%}$. Both round to **54.0%** under the $\pm 0.5\%$ specification tolerance.
- **S30 Post-Intervention Plan (Budget 50 Units)**: **71.7%** (14,269.3 / 19,900 population-weighted reach)
  - *Interventions Chosen*: Backup Generator (20u) + Siren Thoni (10u) + Siren Hill Camp (10u) = 40u total cost.
  - *Verified Simulation Mechanics*: Backup generator restores East Power, bringing Tower B and FM broadcast on-air for all 6 coastal FM villages ($r_{\text{combined}} = 73.75\%$, arrival 0.2 min). Acoustic sirens reach isolated Thoni (arrival 10.06 min, reach 80%) and Hill Camp (arrival 10.55 min, reach 80%). Evaluated via a fresh, un-multiplied simulation run of `simulateS30`.

#### S72 Outage Window (72 Hours, Severe Cyclone Scenario)
- **S72 Baseline (Pre-Intervention)**: **10.6%**
  - *Explicit Timeline*: Hours 0–47 reach is **0.0%** (grid power out, fixed FM transmitter unpowered). At Hour 48, grid power returns under standard recovery, providing 34.4% FM reach for the final 24 hours ($34.4\% \times 24/72 \times 18400/19900 = 10.59\%$).

- **S72 Plan A (Reach Maximiser, Budget 50u)**: **31.8%** overall reach; **Thoni at 0.0%**.
- **S72 Plan B (Fairness-Constrained, Budget 50u)**: **25.0%** overall reach; **lowest village Kovalam at 22.9%** (Thoni protected at 50.0%).
